FRONTEND TEST INVENTORY
Reviewed: 2026-10-05
Total: 63 tests in 9 files

Run from frontend/:
  npm test
Single file example (from frontend/):
  node --test src/data/routes.test.js

Scope: Node built-in test runner with strict assertions against frontend data
helpers, bundled catalog rules, request construction, and navigation actions.
These are not browser end-to-end or rendered React component tests. Navigation
side effects use a browser stub; actual Back/Forward events, component state
retention, CSS appearance, and user clicks in a browser are not covered here.
Lint and production build are separate checks, not entries in this inventory.
Counts refer to named tests; loops exercise additional cases within each test.
Descriptions summarize actual assertions, not a guarantee of all possible cases.
Update this inventory when tests are added, removed, renamed, or changed.

FILE: frontend/src/data/equipmentSave.test.js
Tests: 8

1. replacement costs persist and older Astra saves receive the default
   Behavior and purpose: Round-trips zero and custom costs. Older Astra saves default to 1B and ordinary gear to zero; invalid imported costs are rejected.

2. wrong-job secondaries are rejected on import and removed from saved equipment
   Behavior and purpose: Rejects a Xenon secondary imported for Phantom, drops it during saved-state restoration, and accepts it for Xenon. Protects job eligibility when loading equipment.

3. equipment export/import preserves class, items and recorded rolls
   Behavior and purpose: Round-trips class, two separately equipped rings, stars and limits, modes, safeguards, potentials, flames, flame type, and boss status. Restores the catalog item level.

4. empty equipment exports and UTF-8 BOM imports are supported
   Behavior and purpose: Accepts an export without editable gear and a UTF-8 BOM, restoring the class plus the mandatory fixed Ring of Restraint.

5. malformed imports and invalid rolls are rejected before returning equipment
   Behavior and purpose: Rejects malformed JSON, wrong schema/version/class/slot/item, invalid stars and limits, unsupported modes/safeguards, malformed potential values, invalid flame tiers/types, and nonboolean boss status before returning equipment.

6. imports rebuild catalog fields instead of trusting file metadata
   Behavior and purpose: Replaces imported names, configuration, and icon URLs with trusted bundled catalog values. Imported metadata cannot redefine an item.

7. imports reject class-incompatible weapons and conflicting overall/bottom slots
   Behavior and purpose: Rejects a Hero weapon for Bishop and rejects simultaneous overall/bottom equipment regardless of property order.

8. ring four is fixed across defaults, saved data, imports and class changes
   Behavior and purpose: Checks the fixed default and sole ring-4 choice, blocks replacement, preserves it across class changes, and normalizes missing, old, or edited ring-4 saves/imports without changing ring-1.

FILE: frontend/src/data/equipmentUpgrade.test.js
Tests: 17

9. Xenon requests carry three primary stats and no secondary stats
   Behavior and purpose: Builds a Xenon request with STR, DEX, and LUK as primary stats, no secondary stats, and ATT offense.

10. consecutive Astra steps combine into 0–10 with total costs, gains and efficiency
   Behavior and purpose: Merges ten consecutive steps into a 0-to-10 range, totaling gain 20, cost 5,500, and attempts 55; recalculates gain/cost without mutating the input.

11. grouping preserves interruptions, separate slots, gaps and reversed star order
   Behavior and purpose: Keeps rows separate across intervening items, different ring slots, missing stars, reversed steps, and unavailable values; accepts empty input.

12. Astra 10–15 prerequisites precede higher-efficiency 15–17 upgrades
   Behavior and purpose: Reorders shuffled Astra steps so 10-to-15 prerequisites precede later stars, then groups the continuous 10-to-17 range without changing the input.

13. equal-efficiency choices preserve ranking priority and separate equipped slots
   Behavior and purpose: Breaks efficiency ties using original ranking priority while treating ring-1 and ring-2 independently; accepts an empty queue.

14. six-star lookahead chooses a later payoff over another item and commits its prerequisites
   Behavior and purpose: Chooses a six-step range whose final gain beats another item, includes every prerequisite, and leaves the seventh step until after that competitor; preserves input.

15. lookahead stops at six stars and does not use a seventh-star payoff
   Behavior and purpose: Does not choose a low-star range solely for a payoff seven steps away. The better immediate competitor stays first.

16. range efficiency includes expensive prerequisites instead of averaging per-star ratios
   Behavior and purpose: Ranks total gain divided by total cost; a cheap final upgrade cannot hide an expensive prerequisite.

17. Astra below 15 can win on post-15 gains beyond the normal six-star horizon
   Behavior and purpose: Allows a below-15 item to win through post-15 gains outside the usual six-step horizon, including all 17 steps and their combined cost/gain.

18. post-15 gains do not win when the full prerequisite cost is too high
   Behavior and purpose: Keeps another item first when reaching the post-15 payoff is too expensive after counting prerequisites.

19. lookahead can choose fewer than six stars and reassesses after the chosen range
   Behavior and purpose: Can commit a two-star range, then reconsider other items instead of forcing a six-star block.

20. replacement costs reach requests and grouped boom totals affect ordering
   Behavior and purpose: Requests carry Astra defaults and zero overrides. Grouped ranges sum fractional booms and replacement spending; efficiency and ordering use total cost.

21. upgrade requests preserve duplicate slots and convert recorded rolls to API fields
   Behavior and purpose: Builds separate ring entries with their stars and limits, preserves modes/safeguards and explicit false boss status, converts numeric strings, and omits empty potential rows.

22. upgrade requests map overall and weapon types, retain clean offense and default empty rolls
   Behavior and purpose: Maps overall potential to outfit and weapon potential to weapon, preserves base weapon offense, and defaults missing stars/rolls to zero/empty arrays.

23. saved upgrade settings accept zero weights and recover from invalid values
   Behavior and purpose: Preserves zero as a valid weight while replacing invalid cube type, negative weight, and infinite weight with defaults.

24. upgrade selections survive restoration and reach the API
   Behavior and purpose: Restores valid upgrade categories and passes them to the API, removes obsolete flame type/cost settings, and defaults when every saved category is invalid.

25. fixed ring four is excluded from all upgrade requests
   Behavior and purpose: Ensures requests omit ring-4 while retaining another ring slot, so the fixed skill ring cannot receive enhancement recommendations.

61. full equipment sets larger than a cookie survive storage and restoration
   Behavior and purpose: Saves a real catalog equipment set exceeding 4 KB,
   reloads all classes/settings and equipment, and verifies restored equipment
   matches the serialized save using a local-storage test double.

62. legacy cookies migrate after successful writes and local storage takes precedence
   Behavior and purpose: Recovers an old cookie, writes it to local storage,
   retires the cookie only after success, and prefers local storage over stale
   cookie data on subsequent reads.

   Behavior and purpose: Falls back from malformed local data or blocked storage
